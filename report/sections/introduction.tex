\section{Introduction}

Independent navigation through complex indoor and outdoor environments remains a critical challenge for blind and visually impaired 
individuals. While modern assistive technologies often rely on 2D color image-based scene descriptions, these approaches suffer
 from a lack of precise spatial information. Specifically, they fall short in conveying accurate distances, orientations, and 
 relative positions of environmental features such as obstacles, landmarks, and pathways. As a result, users are often left without
  sufficient spatial context to make safe and confident mobility decisions in dynamic and unfamiliar environments.

Recent advancements in 3D spatial understanding offer promising avenues for enhancing accessibility tools by incorporating 
depth-aware scene interpretation. In particular, systems leveraging 3D data can more effectively describe the geometry and layout
 of a user's surroundings, thereby enabling more informative and actionable guidance.

This research aims to develop and evaluate a 3D spatial scene description system tailored specifically for blind and visually 
impaired smartphone users. The central research question is: \textit{How can a mobile system accurately and interactively describe
 3D spatial scenes to support real-time navigation and obstacle avoidance for blind users?} To address this, we propose a solution
  that integrates Gemini Spatial Understanding capabilities to provide precise, user-centered spatial descriptions and interactive
   audio guidance. The system will be designed to identify key objects, calculate their spatial relationships, and communicate this
    information through accessible audio interfaces to support real-world navigation.

This paper presents the system design, implementation on both laptop and iPhone platforms, and a comprehensive evaluation
 in diverse real-world scenarios including urban navigation, public transport, grocery stores, and object localization.
  Additionally, we explore opportunities for system optimization through model fine-tuning and potential on-device deployment
   strategies.
