# Structure of the Final Presentation

## 1. Introduction
- Motivate and describe the topic/problem.
- List the research questions.


## 2. Background *(Short, single slide)*
- Describe all concepts necessary to understand the report.

## 3. Concept *(Focus)*
- Describe your approach in detail.
- Use diagrams.

## 4. Evaluation *(Focus)*
- Describe the experiments you have conducted.
- Use sensible result visualizations.

## 5. Discussion
- Discuss the experimental results.
- Describe hypotheses that explain the results.

## 6. Timeline
- When happened what?

## 7. Conclusion & Future Work
- Summarize the presentation.
- Describe potential future work.

## 8. Team
- Specify who has contributed to what part of the presentation.
- Specify who has contributed to what part of the overall project.

## 9. References
- List all (figure) references.
