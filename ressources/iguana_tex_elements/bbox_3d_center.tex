3D center computation from bounding box:
$$
d_{\text{center}} = P_{10}\left(\mathcal{D}_{\text{bbox}}\right)
$$

where $P_{10}$ denotes the 10th percentile and $\mathcal{D}_{\text{bbox}}$ contains depth values within the bounding box region:
$$
\mathcal{D}_{\text{bbox}} = \left\{d(v,u) : v_0 \leq v \leq v_1, u_0 \leq u \leq u_1, d(v,u) > 0\right\}
$$

Bounding box center in pixel coordinates:
$$
\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{center}} = \begin{bmatrix}
u_{\text{center}} \\
v_{\text{center}}
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
\frac{u_0 + u_1}{2} \\
\frac{v_0 + v_1}{2}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Final 3D center: $\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{cam}} = \text{pixel\_to\_camera}\left(\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{center}}, d_{\text{center}}, \mathbf{K}\right)$
