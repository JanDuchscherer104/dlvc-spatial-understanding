\textbf{DEPRECATED:} BEV rotation from bounding box geometry (use 3D position instead):

Angular offset from image center:
$$
\theta_{\text{angular}} = \arctan\left(\frac{u_{\text{pixel}} - c_x}{f_x}\right)
$$

Degree conversion with wrapping:
$$
\theta_{\text{deg}} = \begin{cases}
\theta_{\text{angular}} \cdot \frac{180°}{\pi} & \text{if } \theta_{\text{angular}} \geq 0 \\
360° + \theta_{\text{angular}} \cdot \frac{180°}{\pi} & \text{if } \theta_{\text{angular}} < 0
\end{cases}
$$

Clock hour discretization:
$$
h_{\text{clock}} = \begin{cases}
\left\lfloor \frac{\theta_{\text{deg}}}{30°} + 0.5 \right\rfloor \bmod 12 & \text{if result} \neq 0 \\
12 & \text{if result} = 0
\end{cases}
$$

where $\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{pixel}} = [u_{\text{pixel}}, v_{\text{pixel}}]^T$ is the bounding box center and $[c_x, c_y]^T$ is the principal point.
