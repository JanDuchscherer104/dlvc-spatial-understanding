Bird's Eye View (BEV) rotation from 3D camera coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_{\text{bearing}} &= \arctan2\left(x_{\text{cam}}, z_{\text{cam}}\right) \\
\theta_{\text{deg}} &= \theta_{\text{bearing}} \cdot \frac{180°}{\pi} \bmod 360° \\
h_{\text{clock}} &= \left\lfloor \frac{\theta_{\text{deg}}}{30°} + 0.5 \right\rfloor \bmod 12
\end{aligned}
$$

where $\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{cam}} = [x_{\text{cam}}, y_{\text{cam}}, z_{\text{cam}}]^T$ and we use the horizontal plane $(x_{\text{cam}}, z_{\text{cam}})$.

Clock position mapping: $h_{\text{clock}} = 12$ (or 0) corresponds to $\theta_{\text{deg}} = 0°$ (straight ahead in +Z direction).

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_{bearing} &= \text{atan2}(x_{cam}, z_{cam}) \\
\theta_{deg} &= \theta_{bearing} \cdot \frac{180°}{\pi} \bmod 360° \\
\text{clock\_hour} &= \left\lfloor \frac{\theta_{deg}}{30°} + 0.5 \right\rfloor \bmod 12
\end{aligned}
$$

where $(x_{cam}, z_{cam})$ are the camera coordinates in the horizontal plane, and clock hour 12 corresponds to $\theta_{deg} = 0°$ (straight ahead).
