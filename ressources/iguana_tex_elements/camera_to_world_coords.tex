Camera-to-world coordinate transformation:
$$
\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{world}} = \mathbf{T}_{\text{CW}} \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{p}_{\text{cam}} \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

where $\mathbf{T}_{\text{CW}} = \left(\mathbf{T}_{\text{WC}}\right)^{-1} \in \text{SE}(3)$ and $\mathbf{T}_{\text{WC}}$ is the world-to-camera transformation:

$$
\mathbf{T}_{\text{WC}} =
\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf{R}_{\text{WC}} & \boldsymbol{t}_{\text{WC}} \\
\mathbf{0}^T & 1
\end{bmatrix}, \quad
\mathbf{T}_{\text{CW}} =
\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf{R}_{\text{WC}}^T & -\mathbf{R}_{\text{WC}}^T \boldsymbol{t}_{\text{WC}} \\
\mathbf{0}^T & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
