Binary mask erosion for robust depth statistics:
$$
M_{\text{eroded}} = M \ominus \mathbf{S}^{(3)}
$$

where $\mathbf{S}^{(3)} \in \{0,1\}^{3 \times 3}$ is a $3 \times 3$ structuring element and $\ominus$ denotes morphological erosion.

Depth statistics computation from eroded mask:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}} &= \left\{d(v,u) : M_{\text{eroded}}(v,u) > 0 \land d(v,u) > 0\right\} \\
d_{\min} &= \text{nearest\_nonzero}\left(\mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}}\right) \\
d_{\text{med}} &= P_{50}\left(\mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}}\right) \\
d_{\max} &= P_{90}\left(\mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

where $P_k(\cdot)$ denotes the $k$-th percentile operator.

\textbf{Purpose:} Erosion removes boundary pixels to mitigate background depth contamination.
