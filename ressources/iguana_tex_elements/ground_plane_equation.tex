Ground plane equation in camera coordinates:
$$
\boldsymbol{n}^T \boldsymbol{x} + d = 0
$$

RANSAC-based ground plane detection:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{n}_{\text{norm}} &= \frac{\boldsymbol{n}}{\|\boldsymbol{n}\|_2} \\
d_{\text{plane}} &= d
\end{aligned}
$$

Robust normal orientation for camera coordinate system:
$$
\boldsymbol{n}_{\text{final}} = \begin{cases}
\boldsymbol{n}_{\text{norm}} & \text{if } \boldsymbol{n}_{\text{norm}} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{\text{up}} > 0 \land \boldsymbol{n}_{\text{norm}} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{\text{fwd}} > 0 \\
-\boldsymbol{n}_{\text{norm}} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

where $\boldsymbol{u}_{\text{up}} = [0, -1, 0]^T$ (up direction) and $\boldsymbol{u}_{\text{fwd}} = [0, 0, 1]^T$ (forward direction) in camera coordinates.
