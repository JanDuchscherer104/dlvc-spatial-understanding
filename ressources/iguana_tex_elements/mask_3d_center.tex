3D center computation from segmentation mask:
$$
d_{\text{mask}} = \text{nearest\_nonzero}\left(\mathcal{D}_{\text{mask}}\right)
$$

where $\mathcal{D}_{\text{mask}} = \left\{d(v,u) : M(v,u) > 0, d(v,u) > 0\right\}$ and $M(v,u) \in \{0,1\}$ is the binary segmentation mask.

Center of mass from mask (median-based):
$$
\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{center}} = \begin{bmatrix}
u_{\text{center}} \\
v_{\text{center}}
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
\text{median}\left(\left\{u : M(v,u) > 0\right\}\right) \\
\text{median}\left(\left\{v : M(v,u) > 0\right\}\right)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Final 3D center: $\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{cam}} = \text{pixel\_to\_camera}\left(\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{center}}, d_{\text{mask}}, \mathbf{K}\right)$
