Robust depth estimation using two-sided MAD (\textit{Median Absolute Deviation}) for outlier rejection:
$$
d_{\text{robust}} = \min\bigl\{d \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}} : L \le d \le U \bigr\}
\quad\text{where}\quad
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}} &= \{d \in \mathcal{D} : d > 0\},\\
m &= \mathrm{median}\bigl(\mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}}\bigr),\\
\mathrm{MAD} &= \mathrm{median}\bigl(|\mathcal{D}_{\text{valid}} - m|\bigr),\\
L &= m - k_{L} \cdot \mathrm{MAD},\quad U = m + k_{U} \cdot \mathrm{MAD}.
\end{aligned}
$$
