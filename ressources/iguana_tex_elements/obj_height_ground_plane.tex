Object height computation from ground plane:
$$
h_{\text{object}} = \boldsymbol{n}_{\text{ground}}^T \boldsymbol{p}_{\text{center}} + d_{\text{ground}}
$$

where:
\begin{align}
\boldsymbol{n}_{\text{ground}} &\in \mathbb{R}^3 \text{ is the unit normal vector of the ground plane} \\
d_{\text{ground}} &\in \mathbb{R} \text{ is the distance parameter} \\
\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{center}} &\in \mathbb{R}^3 \text{ is the object's 3D center in camera coordinates}
\end{align}

The ground plane equation is $\boldsymbol{n}_{\text{ground}}^T \boldsymbol{x} + d_{\text{ground}} = 0$, so $h_{\text{object}}$ represents the signed distance from the object center to the ground plane.
