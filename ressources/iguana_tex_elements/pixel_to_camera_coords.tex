Pixel-to-camera coordinate transformation:
$$
\boldsymbol{p}_{\text{cam}} =
\begin{bmatrix}
x_{\text{cam}} \\
y_{\text{cam}} \\
z_{\text{cam}}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{(u - c_x) \cdot d}{f_x} \\
\frac{(v - c_y) \cdot d}{f_y} \\
d
\end{bmatrix}
$$

where $(u, v) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ are pixel coordinates, $d \in \mathbb{R}^+$ is depth, and $\mathbf{K} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$ is:
$$
\mathbf{K} =
\begin{bmatrix}
f_x & 0 & c_x \\
0 & f_y & c_y \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
